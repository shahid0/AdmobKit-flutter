# Native template attribution

The native template arrangements are adapted from
[flutter_monetization_kit](https://github.com/DevHamza090/flutter_monetization_kit),
commit `dddc6edb13ee636bcdc436c27da8fc4752321e60` (Android native layouts and
`GoogleNativeAdLayouts.swift`). Layouts are composed using platform stack/linear
layouts and bound through Google Mobile Ads factories in this package; upstream
ad loading, caching, platform views, and styling transport are not included.
